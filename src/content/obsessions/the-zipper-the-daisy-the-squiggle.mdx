Two LE PULL patterns in one week because they are currently doing something. The ICord Sweater is a classic at this point — i-cord applied freeform in loops all over a boxy pullover, in neon yellow with pink or black with white, depending on your mood. The Emma Cardi is newer and does wide colorblock stripes in chunky mohair-blend. They're not the same vibe at all. One is maximalist texture, one is maximalist color. Both are completely correct.

## The scrapbook jacket

Bethany Glover's scrapbook zipper jacket is exactly what the name suggests: every row a different color, worked from whatever you have, finished with a real zipper. Every version I've seen is different. That's the point. It's the pattern that turns your yarn guilt into an outfit.

## The daisy cardigan

Bea Fischer-Harrison's Crazy Daisy Cardigan has a daisy on it. An actual three-dimensional daisy worked in contrasting yarn, petals separate from the center, sewn directly onto the body of the cardigan. Plus a daisy collar. The cardigan itself is a clean simple shape so the daisy has nowhere to hide — it just sits there being a daisy, which is exactly the correct amount of commitment.

## The dot top

p1.zip makes a top called dot|top. Fitted, sleeveless, two-color vertical dot texture — the stitch is the whole thing and the silhouette stays out of the way. Five sizes. It looks like something you'd see on a vintage market rack in exactly the right color combination and then think about for three weeks.
