This is the week it got cold enough to want a real sweater, so there are two proper cable sweaters on the list. There's also a tank top covered in flowers, because I have no self-control.

## The petals

Siiri is a mohair tank knit with two strands held together, so it's basically weightless, and then covered in crocheted flower petals. It came out of Novita's collaboration with the fashion label By Hinders, and it's the most runway thing on this list by a lot. In poppy red it looks like a garden someone decided to wear.

Medokvit takes flowers the more traditional way. Teti Lutsak named it after the Ukrainian words for honey and flower: floral colorwork panels frame a honeycomb texture, and it's built in modules, so you can knit a vest or a full pullover. The project photos are a good reminder of how much color changes a pattern.

## The cables

Deep Winter Sweater is the one to cast on right now. Full cable panels run down the front and back, it's knit in a bulky, airy yarn, and you can choose a crew neck or a fold-over turtleneck. Edith Sweater is the quieter option: one band of knotted cables across the chest of a plain, roomy sweater, like an old fisherman's sweater that lived through the 1980s.

Lullaby is cables too, but not the usual kind. They're I-cord cables sitting on top of a slipped-stitch body, in two colors so they stand right off the fabric. For now it's only in Miki Teragaki's book Knit Pop.

## The Midsommar vest

Hårga is a buttoned slipover striped all over with colorwork. Sara Ottosson based it on the striped fabric of Swedish folk costumes, and named it after Hårga, the village in the folk tale where the devil makes everyone dance until they die. The movie Midsommar borrowed that story too. It comes out October 1st in Laine Nordic Knits 2.

## The everyday ones

Benyuan Light is the lighter-yarn take on Xiaowei's original Benyuan: a clean funnel neck, a ribbed yoke, and lots of drape. Jetty Jumper is Lydia Morrow's cropped drop-shoulder with a horizontal yoke that practically asks for colorblocking. It's sized from 32" to 72" and includes optional bust short rows.
