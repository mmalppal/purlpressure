## The pattern

I won't name it because I don't want this to be a review — but it's the boxy fingering tee with the slightly-wider-than-comfortable neckline that everyone on Instagram has knit twice. You know the one.

## Why I keep coming back

I cast this on for the first time in 2024 in a pale stone linen-blend. Hated it. The neckline gaped, the fabric had no body, the whole thing read as "art teacher who lost their will to live."

Cast it on again last summer in a butter-yellow cotton-silk. Loved the first three inches. Frogged at the underarm because the gauge was so tight my hands hurt.

Cast it on a third time this month in <strong>periwinkle merino-silk fingering</strong>, on bigger needles than the pattern says, and reader. <em>Reader.</em>

> The pattern wasn't the problem. The fabric was the problem. It always is.

## What I learned

A pattern can be perfect and still be wrong for you three times in a row before it's right. That's not the pattern's fault. That's the gauge swatch you didn't take seriously, the yarn you grabbed because it was on sale, the moment you decided the linen would "soften with wear" when you knew it wouldn't.
