Some weeks have a theme. This week the theme is: things that are doing a lot, and one thing that is doing very little on purpose.

Let's start with the Nautical Cardigan from CocoonHMS. There is a lighthouse. There are seagulls. There is a bird wearing a striped shirt. The bird appears to be a sailor. The whole cardigan tells a story and I mean that in the best possible way. It's intarsia that commits fully to the bit.

The Lumber Sweater by Mette Christensen (The Knit is On) is a plaid sweater. I have been on the fence about it all week and I keep landing on the orange and cream colorway being completely correct. The blue-brown version exists for people with more self-control than me.

## The vest situation

Two vests this week and they could not be more different.

The Sugar Sweet Woven Vest by Nancy Marchant looks like someone took every color they owned and wove them together and it worked. It should feel chaotic. It doesn't. The brioche construction holds it all in place.

The Eggcellent Vest by Brenda Calota has fried eggs on it. Embroidered yolks on a black and cream base. There's a checkerboard version too. I don't know what I expected from Brenda Calota but this is exactly right.

## The rest

The Crooked Cardi from Simone Bertin is chartreuse with a safety pin closure and a cropped length. Every decision was intentional. All of them were correct.

Sofia Cardigan from Mochiknits: red, collared, no explanation needed. Either you're a red cardigan person or you're not.

The Vara Sweater by Sofia Scotford is the calm in this roundup. Cream, ribbed, a vertical panel that breaks it up slightly. Wear it with everything. Knit it fast.
