This week astronomers said they'd worked out the actual sky van Gogh was looking at when he painted The Starry Night. Other astronomers said not so fast. I don't have a side. I cut the painting into eight pieces and put a sweater on each one. Swipe through and you walk across the whole thing.

## The butter

BIG STICK OF BUTTER is a sweater that is a stick of butter. The pattern comes with charts for salted and unsalted, which is the kind of decision I respect.

## The belt

Izzy Top is a tunic with an asymmetrical hem and a belt you knit to match. It's sized XXS to 9XL.

## The Starry Night

Softis isn't a Starry Night sweater, but it's the one that looks the most like a painting. The stripes are so thin they blend together from across the room.

## The rest

Sideline is a crewneck with a sportswear stripe running down the arms. Daisy Chain puts daisies around the yoke. Jasmine Jacket has a collar you could hide in. Joha Loop shades its yoke and runs stripes down the raglan lines. Alphonse ends in lace at the hem and cuffs.

The prices are written on the moon, the real one. The faces belong to project knitters who didn't sign up to be in my carousel, so they're covered with stars from the painting.
