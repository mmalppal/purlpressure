Two patterns this week don't technically exist yet — Sawtooth Jacket lands September 7th, Hap Quilt September 1st — and they're both doing the exact same trick: piecing mitered squares together like a quilt block and calling it a garment. I'd normally save something like that for its own week. Instead I found a whole flock of sheep and decided everything could just go in together.

## The patchwork

Sawtooth Jacket and Hap Quilt are the two to pre-order your yarn for now, because neither is actually available yet. Sawtooth pieces mitered squares into eight-pointed stars across a full jacket with a wooden toggle closure — it looks like a quilt that got ambitious. Hap Quilt does the quieter version of the same idea: one big mitered block, dead center, front and back of a simple vest, from Gudrun Johnston's upcoming Shetland book.

## The colorwork

The Sheep Sweater is exactly what it says — a full flock marching around the yoke in stranded colorwork, and every sample photo styles it differently enough that it doesn't read as a novelty sweater until you actually look. Juniper Pullover is the quiet one of the week, a lavender-olive gradient yoke with a small cross motif fading through it. Alder Sweater sits between the two: a tight two-color texture stitch that reads like tweed from across a room and changes completely between colorways.

## The details

Deania Cardigan's whole personality is the collar — cropped, button-front, with a Peter Pan collar big enough to matter. Ella Wrap Shirt skips a real closure altogether and ties itself shut in bows at each shoulder. Heirloom Jumper goes the other direction entirely, all vertical eyelet lace on a fitted mock-neck that looks pulled from an actual heirloom, not just named after one.
