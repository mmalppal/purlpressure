Everything this week is either loud or quiet, nothing in between. Two stripe projects that use up the whole bin, three tops doing something structural at the neckline, and one sweater with sleeves that belong to a different garment entirely.

## The stripes

Mix Tape Tee is the one I keep coming back to. Vertical stripes, boxy shape, and it's designed around leftovers — the version knit in full rainbow and the version knit in black-and-cream are the same pattern and I would not have guessed it. Simultaneous Sweater goes denser, stacking and crossing color until the fabric reads almost woven. Both are a good argument for never throwing out a partial skein again.

## The necklines

Artemis Top's cowl pools instead of lying flat, which is the entire pattern. moss in the clouds top goes the other direction — button placket straight down the front, then a small ruffle at the hem to undercut the whole thing. Gibson Sweater splits the difference with a wide scoop and a tie at the throat.

## The texture

Waterbender has a plain ribbed body and then sleeves knit in a honeycomb so deep it looks structural. eddy tee keeps its detail up top with a leaf-and-eyelet yoke that fans across the shoulders, and Carrie Tee runs quiet vertical panels the whole length with bobbles hidden in them.
