It's Halloweentown season, so this week everything is watching you, holding your hand, or wearing a face.

## The eyes

Eye See You Sweater puts a ring of eyes around the yoke. It comes in sizes from 35 to 53 inches, and it looks just as unsettling in pink as it does in burgundy.

## The hands

I Want to Hold Your Hand Scarf is a cabled scarf with a knitted hand at each end, red nails included. It shares a slide with GOJI, a bubbly hood-cowl that sits somewhere between a shawl and a costume.

## The frogs

Dracoclava is a green dragon balaclava with spines down the back. Frog Balaclava has googly eyes and a zipper for a mouth. Both cost less than a coffee.

## The rest

Arachne Pullover hides a spider cable down each sleeve. Spirally is a turtleneck whose ribs spiral down from the neck. Rhea Pullover is all-over texture, shown here in a very loud chartreuse. Endellion has colorwork motifs so big the sizes go up in 20 cm jumps. And Slinky Slinky Sly Sweater is covered in snakes.
