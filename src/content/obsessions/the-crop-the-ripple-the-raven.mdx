This week split itself into two clean piles almost by accident: sweaters that hold a shape, and sweaters that tell a story in color. I didn't plan it that way, but once I saw it I couldn't unsee it.

## The shapes

X90 Sweater is cropped and stays cropped — same silhouette on every model, which is its own kind of confidence. Flutterby Top is the argument against it: sleeveless, draped, photographed mid-movement every single time, like the fabric refuses to sit still for the camera.

## The texture

Ripple Loop Sweater turns the usual vertical cable sideways, so the whole body reads like a wave caught mid-roll. Floks vest scatters a fleck texture across a sleeveless layering piece — quiet from a distance, busy up close. Bella Blocking skips texture and charts entirely and just blocks contrast panels, which turns out to be its own kind of technique.

## The color story

Conspiracy of Ravens sweater goes almost all shadow — dark, dense, more silhouette than pattern. The Good Ol' Hockey Sweater goes the other way with a straight red-white-navy stripe and no reinvention, which is exactly why it works, and it's this designer's first published pattern. Margit closes the week with Arne & Carlos doing a proper colorwork yoke through Rowan, with the widest size range of anything on the list.
