Three Jen Geigley patterns in one week. I counted. I'm not apologizing. She has a particular approach — slightly chaotic, very intentional, deeply committed to the idea that a sweater is allowed to have a *position* — and I find it very hard to look away.

This week also features a scarf shaped like a bloody saw, a hat made of hair rollers, and the Bikini Slipover, which I mean completely sincerely.

## The hair roller hat

Desperate Housefrau is a *free* pattern by Astor Tsang. It is a hat that looks like a head full of hair rollers — each curler individually knitted and attached, in different sizes, with the little picks sticking out. The Ravelry photos include a dog wearing one. One size. I've saved this, unsaved it, and saved it again. It lives in my queue permanently now.

## The Bikini Slipover

Festnetz designed a vest with a bikini top worked into the colorwork. The "bikini" is a colorwork motif — two triangles, some ties — but it's fully committed and completely sincere. I've seen it made in navy, green, red, and cream. The cream one looks like a trompe-l'oeil of a person wearing a bikini under a vest. Sized XS/S through XL/2XL. I'm including this because it's correct.

## The saw

Regina Rioux's scarf is shaped like a handsaw. It has knitted blood dripping from the teeth. It's one size. It's a real pattern you can buy and knit and wear around your neck in public. Horror knitting is a genre and it has always been a genre and it deserves more credit than it gets.

## Warmthness

The third Jen Geigley in the rotation. Big boxy sweater, very slouchy, with large rectangular exterior pockets. The pattern note says "if you could turn those rectangles into pockets…" — reader, she turned the rectangles into pockets. Sized 38–72". I want this in a deep olive.
