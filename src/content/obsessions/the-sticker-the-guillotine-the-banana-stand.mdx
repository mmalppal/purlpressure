Tuesday is Election Day, so this week's picks all have some opinions. The price tags are wax seals, and the project knitters got VOTE buttons.

## The sticker

Erica Jensen votes by mail, which means no I Voted sticker, so she knit one. It takes under 2 grams of scrap yarn and an old gift card you're willing to sacrifice.

## The guillotine

Functional Guillotine comes with a knit head and hats for different seasons. It's listed under softies.

## The banana stand

Arrested Development Knit Mittens say "There's always money" on one hand and "in the banana stand" on the other. George Bluth would want you to read them together.

## The rest

Melt the ICE Hat comes from the red hats Norwegians wore against the Nazi occupation, and all proceeds go to immigrant aid agencies. The ResistHENce Hat is the same idea at chicken scale. PANEM is the cowl from Catching Fire. Shut Up And Take My Money! puts Fry on your chest with the cash out. Mouse Trap is a scarf made of felted cheese.

The faces belong to project knitters who didn't sign up to be in my carousel, so they're covered with VOTE buttons. Go vote.
