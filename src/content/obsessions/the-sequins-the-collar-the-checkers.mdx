Lea (kisskiss.rhinestones) makes a sequined slip top called **Top3000**. Not "sparkle cami," not "gem top" — Top3000. As if there are 2,999 other tops in the system and this one simply scored the highest. It's a fitted cami with a flared hem and it comes in nine sizes and every version of it in the project pages looks like someone made exactly the right decision.

## The Havanese situation

Maija Pihlajaoja makes two separate patterns: the Havanese Collar and the Havanese Cuffs. Both are fluffy boucle or faux fur accessories that you wear *over* your existing clothes — the collar sits around your neck on top of whatever jacket you already own, the cuffs slip over the sleeves. They're one size each. You could wear them over a puffer coat, over a denim jacket, over a boring work blazer. The transformation is not subtle. The name is perfect because the result does look like you're wearing a Havanese.

## Scrappy Checkers

Erin Guimond's Scrappy Checkers is in Pom Pom Quarterly and it is exactly what it sounds like: a colorwork pullover designed to use up your scraps. The stitch pattern is a stranded checker grid, so every color you add is intentional-looking even when you're just using whatever's left in the bin. The size range goes to 68.75 inches. It is the correct answer to the question of what to do with all those partial skeins.

## One more thing

The Brioche Crochet Half-Zip Pullover by Leigh Thayer is crochet, not knitting. I'm including it anyway because brioche crochet looks like knitted brioche and the silhouette — chunky, high half-zip, textured all over — is exactly right for what this stitch wants to be.
