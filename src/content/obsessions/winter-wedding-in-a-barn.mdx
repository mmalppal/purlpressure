## The brief

I want a sweater I can wear over a slip dress in a barn that has, generously, three working space heaters. Long enough to layer, structured enough to not look like a robe, in a color that reads as <em>chosen</em> and not <em>safe.</em>

That last bit is where most barn-wedding knit posts go off the rails.

## The pairings

A few ideas pulled from this week's saved-stash chaos:

- A heavy cardigan in <strong>oxblood-pink</strong> — the slightly muted, slightly weird red that reads warm in candlelight and dramatic in flash
- A worsted pullover in <strong>dusty blush</strong> with a faintly sheen-y silk content, so it picks up the one warm bulb in the rafters
- A mohair-laden cropped cardigan in <strong>walnut brown</strong> over a deep navy slip — boring on paper, a quiet menace in person

> The trick with barn-wedding knits is treating them like outerwear that happens to be soft. Not <em>cozy</em>. Not <em>chunky knit</em>. Outerwear.
