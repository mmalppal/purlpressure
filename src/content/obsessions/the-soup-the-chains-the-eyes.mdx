I opened my Ravelry saves this week and found three Lindsay Degen patterns, a soup sweater, and a tee designed to look like it's wearing another tee. I have no explanation. I just save what I save.

## The Vegetable Soup Sweater

Abigail Gray Designs made a sweater that says SOUP on it. Multiple times. In the intarsia. There are also carrots. A tomato. What I'm fairly certain is a potato. It's the most earnest garment I've encountered this year and I mean that completely as a compliment.

The correct yarn for this is something slightly fluffy, slightly rustic — a light DK wool — so the vegetables have warmth and the SOUP text reads as a declaration rather than a label.

## Lindsay Degen, three times

I don't plan these things. She just keeps making patterns I can't look away from.

The Chains Sweater has literal three-dimensional knitted chain links built into the fabric. The Passing Through Sweater is a dense two-color mosaic structure that reads like woven fabric from a distance. The Eyes Sweater has all-over embroidered eyes in multiple colorways and they are all looking at you with varying levels of concern.

All three in one week. I'm not sorry.

## The Tee in a Tee situation

Sofia Holck Petersen made a tee with a smaller contrasting striped tee built into the yoke. It looks like the sweater put another, smaller sweater on underneath. Or like the sweater got cold. Either reading is correct.
