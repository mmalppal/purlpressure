It's March 29th and I'm already thinking about summer knits. Not in a desperate way. In a *strategic* way.

The case for casting on a tee right now instead of in June: you will actually finish it before the heat becomes a physical threat. You will not be knitting fingering weight cotton in a July sweat spiral, hating yourself, hating the yarn, hating the whole enterprise. March casting on equals June wearing. Do the math.

This week's round-up is heavy on the tee shapes, the dress shapes, and the coordinated set shapes — all the things that require a lighter gauge and a longer runway than most of us admit when we pick them up.

## The one I cannot stop thinking about

The Wrinkle Top by Mingbi Luo. It's dimensional. It's a halter. It's doing something with the fabric that I can't fully explain but deeply respect. The texture looks like what happens when you scrunch a linen shirt and just... leave it that way. On purpose. As a garment.

I don't know if I'll ever actually make this. I know that I think about it constantly.

## The one that's actually reasonable

The Hathor Tee is the easy one in this roundup — diamond lace panel, relaxed shape, works in almost any DK-weight cotton. It's the kind of pattern that photographs modestly and then shows up in person looking like you made a real decision.

## The matching set situation

The Hällevik blouse and skirt from Wool & Beyond are both *free* on Ravelry. A matching knit set. I'm going to make both in a burnt orange linen and I'm not taking questions.
