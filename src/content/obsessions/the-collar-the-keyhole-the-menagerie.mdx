This week is fair isle heavy — four of the eight patterns are doing dense colorwork bands — but the pieces holding my attention are the ones breaking that pattern: a collar, a cutout, and a sweater that's basically an illustration.

## The graphic

Sound Sweater's shawl collar is doing more work than the rest of the sweater combined — fold it down and it reads like outerwear. Alice Sweater runs a checkerboard triangle straight down from the V-neck before handing off to plain stripes, and it changes completely between the charcoal and teal versions. Heritage Pullover skips texture and color rhythm altogether and just embroiders a small farm's worth of animals and objects into a grid — horse, heart, house, sheep, no two squares matching.

## The fair isle

Mona Sweater, Repovesi, and Anemone Sweater are all doing some version of the same thing — dense horizontal banding with a repeating motif — and it's genuinely interesting how different they read. Mona goes bold and floral, Repovesi keeps it warm and geometric with little crosses breaking up the stripes, and Anemone goes small-scale and busy enough that it reads as texture from a distance. Anemone also has more finished projects behind it than anything else on this list by a wide margin.

## The extras

Ask Me Bandana is knit sideways in garter so it drapes like woven fabric instead of sitting flat — worn as a bandana or pulled over the whole head, it's the cheapest and fastest thing here. Frija Blouse is the plain one on purpose: boxy, drop-shoulder, one keyhole cutout at the throat carrying the entire design.
