Brenda Calota brought her goose back this week. He has a cowboy hat now. He was always going to have a cowboy hat. I don't know why I'm surprised.

But first: the Vivienne tank by Svetlana Volkova. Dark plum, ribbed, buttons up the front. If you've been looking for a tank pattern that feels like a real garment — not a placeholder, not a quick summer filler — this is it. The color in every photo is doing so much work it should be paid separately.

The Axis Cardigan from Spektakelstrik has a spine of buttons running down the back and two colorways that couldn't be more different: a deep maroon and a red-and-white stripe that is aggressively fun. Both are correct. The stripe version is the one I'm thinking about at 2am.

## The pillow

Yes, I included a pillow. The Dachshund by Karola Ernstdóttir is a knitted cushion in a blue and cream slip stitch that looks like structured cord fabric. I don't only curate wearables. I curate things that stop me mid-scroll. This stopped me.

## Back to clothes

The Sim Blouse by Simone Bergh is a cream top with a drawstring waist. Understated, wearable, the kind of thing that photographs modestly and then looks exactly right when you actually put it on.

To Draw by Rievive is the chunky cream sweater for when you want something that knits up fast and goes straight onto your body. Slightly oversized, relaxed. Done.

The CPH Sweater by Lilly Schwarzkopf (lesdillesducoeur) is vertical stripes with real volume — three colorways shown in red, navy, and grey. The red one is the correct answer and I'm not entertaining debate on that.

## Mr. Goostavo

He's back. Cowboy hat. Three colorways: pink, burnt orange, sage green. The orange version might be my favorite thing I've seen this month. Brenda Calota does not come to play.
