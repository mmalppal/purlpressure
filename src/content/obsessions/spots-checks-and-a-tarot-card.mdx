The checkerboard trend has been declared over approximately four times in the past two years. Every time someone writes the obituary, three more people cast one on. I refuse to stop saving them. This is my hill.

This week: checkerboards, spots, graphic yokes, a fluffy jacket situation, and a tarot card.

## The one I keep going back to

The Betty Jacket by Le Pull. It's a long-pile fluffy jacket — like if your grandmother's coat and a yeti had a fashion moment together. The pattern photos show it in an optimistic butter yellow, cropped and slightly boxy in exactly the right proportions.

I want to make this in the most aggressive color I can find in a brushed alpaca. I have no current yarn for this. I am already planning the yarn.

## Fortuna's Wheel

The thing about Elspeth Vance's Fortuna's Wheel Pullover is that the checkerboard isn't static — it warps and bends at the raglan seams in a way that makes the whole thing look like it's in motion. Op-art knitting. The matching Trousers are also a pattern. I have questions about my commitment level and the answer is apparently unlimited.

## The tarot one

Tamara Jordan's Tarot Card Sweater lets you pick your card. The Hermit. The Star. The Moon. There's also a version with yarn and needles — the Knitter, presumably. The intarsia panel sits centered on the chest and the sweater is sized 30–62" bust, which is some of the widest size range I've seen on an indie pattern. I want The Hermit in a deep slate blue.
