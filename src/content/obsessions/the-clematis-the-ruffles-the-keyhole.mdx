red bean dream makes a sweater called my clematis sweater. Lowercase, possessive, named after a flower. It's covered in a looping vine cable that wraps around the body like it grew there. It's on Ko-fi, not Ravelry — which means you have to want it enough to go find it, and you will.

## The ruffle situation

lesfillesducoeur and Lilly Schwarzkopf made a mohair bolero that is approximately 40% sleeves. The ruffles start at the elbow and go. They come in white, they come in red, they come in a sheer wine version that is fully unwell. It's one size, which means the drama is non-negotiable, which is actually better. You're not picking a ruffle level. There's only one level.

## Sungem Sweater

2 Bird Knits made the Sungem Sweater for gradient yarn. The body is one color. The sleeves are where the gradient lives — you're watching it transition from one thing to something else the whole time you wear it. It comes in two fit options (feminine and masculine) with ranges up to 5X. The colorways people are using in their project pages are very good.

## Beijing Top

A Pilots Daughter Travels's Beijing Top has a keyhole. That's it. That's the detail. The rest is a clean fitted long-sleeve with a high neck, sized XS through 5XL, and there's a small oval cutout at the very center front. Every version I've seen is in black because of course it is. It's the correct choice for a top that has exactly one thing going on and wants you to notice it.
