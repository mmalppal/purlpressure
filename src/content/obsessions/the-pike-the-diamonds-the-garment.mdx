Before the sweaters: to Jane Doe at Cornell, I believe you. That's all I'm going to say about it here, except that RAINN is free, confidential and open 24/7 at 1-800-656-4673 or [rainn.org](https://rainn.org).

This week went Nordic.

## The pike

Hauki is covered in pike, which is what the name means. It's in Jenna K.'s Knitted Kalevala III, out November 1, so you can look but you can't buy it on its own yet.

## The diamonds

Colman Diamond Sweater is all big patchwork diamonds and comes in 21 sizes, which is more sizes than most patterns have colors. Jeju Sweater does a smaller diamond, one band of it across the middle of a short, boxy body.

## The garment

The Garment is a 2,400-yard turtleneck poncho designed to swallow a six-foot person whole. The designer describes it as something to "experience its heft and splendor," and the project photos agree.

## The rest

Button Me Up Cardi Chunky is a short-sleeved cardigan with a line of red buttons down the front and very little else, which is the point. Ciro is a plain pullover with side slits that people keep knitting in every color there is. Luminen has a fan-and-feather lace panel down the front in yarn so chunky it's a weekend project. And Flowers Sweater is two-color leaves from hem to neck.

Project knitters' faces are covered on the slides because they didn't sign up to be in my carousel.
