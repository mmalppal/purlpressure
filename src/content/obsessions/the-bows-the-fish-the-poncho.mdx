This week went to the woods. The prices are written on tree slices and the faces are covered with pinecones.

## The bows

Jingjing Cardigan closes with three bows down the front instead of buttons. That's the whole idea and it's enough.

## The fish

PIKE is a sweater with one large pike on it. It's the second pike sweater in two weeks, which I didn't plan but am not going to apologize for.

## The poncho

Norma Poncho is a turtleneck poncho with fringe, sized from XS/S to 2XL/3XL. It's the kind of thing you wear on a porch with coffee.

## The rest

Djo is a Fair Isle yoke that uses every color at once. Blue in Green is a big, comfortable pullover with deep pockets, and it's only in Kate Davies' Rip It Up club for now. Icord Raglan runs i-cord stripes down the raglan lines. Advent Sweater blocks its sleeves in contrasting colors. Leaves from the Vine is leaf lace from top to bottom.

Project knitters' faces are covered with pinecones because they didn't sign up to be in my carousel.
