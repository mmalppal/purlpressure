Some weeks the theme is obvious. Some weeks you look at your saved folder and the only honest title is *all of it, I want all of it.*

This is the second kind of week.

## The X-Files one

I usually make a point of only featuring size-inclusive patterns. KT Simmons-Uvin's X-Files sweater comes in one size — 19" across, 24.5" long from the shoulder. I included it anyway because I simply could not help myself. I WANT TO BELIEVE.

The print is the purple desert landscape with the hovering saucer and the iconic text, rendered in duplicate stitch and worsted weight. Someone did this. It exists. I couldn't not include it.

## The romanesco situation

Ranti Studio makes a jumper and a bag that look like romanesco broccoli. If you've ever looked at a romanesco and thought *this is the most beautiful naturally-occurring fractal I've ever seen and I need to wear it* — first of all, same. Second, the pattern exists.

The bag is there too, in case the jumper alone isn't enough of a statement.

## The duck

The Daisy Rollneck by Miranda Copeland has one small embroidered duck on the chest. That's it. A simple rollneck with a duck. XS–5XL. The duck is not making eye contact. The duck has a life to get back to.

## The Ballistics Bib

Lydia Morrow made a collar-bib with intarsia panels. One version says GO! GO! with a lightning bolt and a rocket. Another version is a full Ouija board layout. One size. It snaps at the back and sits like a fancy collar over whatever you're wearing underneath. I don't have a frame for this and I don't need one.
