Bea Fischer-Harrison's Tattoo Flash Cardigan is exactly what it sounds like: swallows, roses, daggers, banners — an entire flash sheet worked in colorwork across a cardigan. It is not subtle and it is not trying to be. Sizes XS through 5XL, every project photo more committed than the last.

## The bike path

Postcard Knits' Bike Path Sweater is a straightforward Breton-stripe pullover in blue and oatmeal — relaxed, easy, correct in every colorway. Someone in the project pages put it on their dog. The dog looked good.

## Snow Gum

Susanna Kaartinen named a colorwork pullover after eucalyptus bark, and the yoke pattern actually reads like peeling bark when you look at it — mottled bands in warm browns and cream. It's the kind of name that sounds decorative until you see the sweater and realize it's literal.

## 11:11

Logan Laine's 11:11 is one pattern, two completely different garments depending on which sample you build: negative ease with bust darts, or positive ease with none. Same shape on paper, entirely different fit in practice. Fits a 30–60 inch bust range either way.
