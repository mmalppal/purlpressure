Bea Fischer-Harrison's Tattoo Flash Cardigan has a snarling wolf head worked in colorwork clear across the back, teeth and all, plus daggers and hearts scattered down the sleeves. Every project swaps in different flash art, so no two look alike, but they're all equally committed. Sizes XS through 5XL.

## The bike path

Postcard Knits' Bike Path Sweater is a wavy cable line running the length of a boxy pullover — but the projects don't agree on anything else. Solid olive, magenta, a two-tone diagonal colorblock in rust and black. Same pattern, completely different sweater every time.

## Snow Gum

Susanna Kaartinen named a button vest after eucalyptus bark, and the scattered vertical-dash motif across the chest genuinely reads like peeling bark when you squint. Works layered over a button-down or on its own, and looks as good oversized on a guy as it does fitted.

## 11:11

Logan Laine's 11:11 is a fitted turtleneck built entirely around a vertical ombré gradient — every color transition in the skein becomes a deliberate stripe. The wilder the gradient, the more the sweater looks like it was dyed specifically for this pattern, even when it wasn't.
