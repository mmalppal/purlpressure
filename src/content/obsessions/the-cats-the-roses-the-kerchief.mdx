Two books drop this week and both have something on this list. The rest you can buy tonight.

## The cats

Crazy Cats Pullover has a yoke full of cats caught mid-fall, legs everywhere. The designer owns two cats and says she wanted the yoke "more on the crazy side," and she got there. It looks as good in navy and rust as it does in the loud chartreuse.

## The roses

White Roses is a plain black cardigan until you embroider it. A trail of white roses runs down each front, and the pattern comes with the diagram and the crewel wool colors. It's in Claudia Quintanilla's book *Threads of Memory*, out October 1.

## The kerchief

Aida Kerchief is a basketweave triangle with a crocheted edge and a cross-stitched flower on top. The pattern includes a blank version too, so you can stitch whatever you want onto it.

## The rest

Ease In Tee is a short-sleeved raglan with braids tracing the raglan lines, and it's 15% off through October 2. As If Pullover makes its plaid by slipping stitches to the front, and yes, it's named for Clueless. Plain Yoke Poncho is stockinette all the way down and comes with a novice edition. Mai & Juin are a matching top and skirt in basket rib, both in Rui Yamamuro's *Common Thread*, out October 2. And 06 Pooh's Sweater says "Doing nothing often leads to the very best something" across the front, in mohair, which hides your mistakes.
