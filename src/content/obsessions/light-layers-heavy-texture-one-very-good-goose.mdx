This week went in seven directions at once, which is basically on brand.

The Plumetis by Julia Knits In Paris is first and it should be. The stitch is like a woven fabric that somehow happened with two needles. It reads sheer in some lights, structured in others. I've been staring at it for three days.

If you want texture but aren't ready to commit to cables, the Jenny Blouse from PetiteKnit is your bridge pattern. Basket weave that's readable, not fussy. The sage colorway in every photo is doing a lot of the persuading.

Blouse No. 3 from My Favourite Things is the anti-statement statement piece. Fitted, simple, short sleeve. You knit it and then you wear it every week for the rest of the season. That's the whole thing.

## The goose socks

I need to address the Oh Goose! Socks because yes, I included socks this week and I would do it again. Halyna Mykhalchenko (@socks_by_halyna) has a 3D sculpted goose coming out of the cuff. The goose has a beak. The goose is standing up. I don't have a logical argument for why I need these — I just do.

## The rest of the round-up

The Haven Harbor Slipcover Vest from Ashley Lillis has a silhouette that reads effortless. Drop shoulder, slightly boxy, the kind of vest that looks expensive without trying.

The Outline Raglan from Jessie Maed Designs: it's the blue. The blue is doing everything. The rest of the construction is good too but honestly, the blue.

City Tivoli by Ingunn Birkeland is a check yoke that I keep almost skipping and then not skipping. Colorful without being chaotic.

The Scribble Top by Alice Hoyle is open-work for people who are thinking about summer with actual intention. The structure of it is smart.
