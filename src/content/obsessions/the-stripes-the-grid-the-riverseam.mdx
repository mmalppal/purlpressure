Hot week, so the whole list skews sleeveless and short-sleeve. Three colorblocked pieces, two grids, one riverbed running down a sleeve, and a set that's completely free.

## The colorblocking

A Shirt of Fire and Ice does exactly what it says — half the shirt runs warm, half runs cool, and the line between them is the whole design. The Knitted Riverseam Sweater does something similar but softer, a seam tracing down the sleeve like it's following a current. Joelentine tee splits into four quadrants instead of two, waffle-stitched, so every version reads like a different flag.

## The grids

Griddy is a windowpane check knit flat into a boxy tee — deceptively simple, wildly size-inclusive, and it comes in more colorways than I can count. Pair it with the Aventine Tank's keyhole neckline and you've got two ways to wear the same idea: pattern doing the work, or a single detail doing the work.

## The free one

Relaxed Arrow Set is a whole tank-and-pant set, free, with a chevron lace border at every hem. There is no reason not to have already cast this on.
