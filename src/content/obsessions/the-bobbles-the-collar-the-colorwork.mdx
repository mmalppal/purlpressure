Autumn arrived in my saved folder about three weeks before it arrived outside. Everything here is warmer, heavier, and more covered up than what I was looking at in July.

## The Laine problem

Four of these — Worker, Ihana, Hestia, and Ljung — are all from Laine No. 30. It's print only, there's no digital download code, and it doesn't publish until August 27. So I get to look at them for three more weeks and do nothing about it. The issue is called *Layers*, which feels pointed.

## The texture

Melmo is bobbles and open stitchwork scattered across a drop-shoulder body, thick enough that the fabric reads as pattern rather than as stitches. Hestia goes the other way, keeping the body plain so the cream colorwork medallions across the yoke get the whole stage. Ljung stacks dotted colorwork bands the entire length of the sweater — stripes from across the street, something much more involved once you're close.

## The quiet ones

Ihana is a cropped cardigan whose entire personality is a soft rounded collar. Mia Blouse is a mock neck with a short button placket and nothing else going on, which is the point. Trame Slipover is open mesh, sleeveless, and €5.50, so it's the one I'll probably actually cast on. And Mara Blouse is Breton stripes written for both DK and 4-ply, which is a nicer gift than it sounds.
