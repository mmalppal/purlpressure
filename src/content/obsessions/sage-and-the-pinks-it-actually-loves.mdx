## The problem with sage + pink

Sage is one of the most-used colors in indie knitting right now and it is being <em>wronged</em> daily. The instinct is to pair it with millennial pink because they're both "muted" — but they're muted in totally different ways. Sage is cool-leaning yellow-green; millennial pink is warm-cool-confused beige-pink. Together they go flat.

## What sage actually wants

Three pinks that bring sage to life instead of dampening it:

- **Oxblood pink** — same temperature, more guts. Wears like a deep plum from across the room.
- **Dusty rose** — the one with a touch of grey in it. Reads vintage. Reads <em>composed.</em>
- **Blush with a warm undertone** — only if there's enough chroma in your sage to hold its own. Otherwise it'll wash you both out.
