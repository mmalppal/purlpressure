This week ran heavy on colorwork with a trick up its sleeve. Three of these do something your eye has to work to resolve — the shadow goldfish that only appear at an angle, the optical illusion mosaic, the night-and-day cats that swap light for dark across the centerline.

## The storm

Villasilmu's Baltic Storm Sweater is the one I keep going back to. It's a full seascape — banded waves, whitecaps of foam, and a single red lighthouse standing in the middle of it. The colorwork does the thing where a garment stops being a garment and becomes a little painting you can wear. Sized all the way to 5XL, too.

## The cats

The Night & Day Cats Sweater splits straight down the middle: a pale cat leaping across the dark half, a dark cat across the light half. It's the kind of clever that could read as gimmicky and instead just reads as good.

## The illusions

Shadow knitting is one of those techniques that feels like a magic trick — the Shadow Goldfish Top looks like plain stripes head-on, then reveals a school of goldfish the moment you tilt it. Pair it with Hanna Lejon's Optical Illusion Sweater, a mosaic that seems to move, and you've got a whole week built on knits that don't hold still.
