Cold week. Everything on this list is a sweater, a jacket, or something you put on over a sweater, and I'm not apologizing for it.

## The side zip

Singapore Side Zip Jacket is the one I keep going back to. It's a zip-front jacket with a stand collar, and instead of picking a size off a chart you knit it to your own measurements. That's a different kind of pattern to work through, more math up front and no grading to argue with, but it's also the reason nobody has to ask whether they're at the top or bottom of the range.

## The checkerboard

Chess Pullover is a checkerboard worked in a single color, so the pattern lives entirely in the texture. It reads as a flat gray sweater from across the room and then turns into a whole grid when you get close. Sized XS to 4X.

Soigné Jacket is the other structured one: cropped, contrast collar, a two-color stitch that comes out looking like tweed. It's the piece here most likely to get a "where's that from."

## The bobbles

Hawthorne is a sleeveless mock-turtleneck covered in bobbles, and it's meant to be worn with ten to twenty inches of positive ease. The smallest finished bust is 51 inches. It's not a mistake, it's the point.

## The rest

Wax & Wane is all-over colorwork in a scalloped moon-phase repeat, and the project photos do more selling than I can. Georgia Sweater is vertical stripes on something soft, in every color combination knitters could think of. Cameron Sweater is a plain wide-neck turtleneck that fits up to a 69.75 inch bust. Primrose Sweater is tweedy, crewnecked, and decorated with exactly one row of eyelets.
