There are two stripe sweaters this week. They are not doing the same thing.

**Rayas Pullover** by The Wandering Flock is clean and geometric — wide horizontal stripes, boxy fit, the kind of sweater that works in literally every color combination and still has a point of view. **Theo Sweater** by kolibri by johanna is mohair-blend stripes, which means the edges blur and the whole thing softens into something almost painterly. They're stripe sweaters for different moods. You could probably want both at once.

## The fish tin

Katelyn Nguyen's Omega 3 Sweater has a sardine tin on it. Appliquéd colorwork, worked as a separate piece and sewn onto the chest. There are project pages in cream, in blue, in a very correct terracotta red. It sounds fiddly. It is probably fiddly. Every single project page suggests it is completely worth it. This is the kind of detail that makes a sweater feel like a decision.

## Franka Top

BitteMachDas's Franka Top is a ruffled mohair cami that was specifically designed to wear over a long-sleeve tee. Not as a standalone, not as a layering piece that happens to have ruffles — as the deliberate outer layer of a thought-out outfit. The mohair makes the ruffles look almost cloudlike. The yellow one on the cover is doing everything right.

## Alma

Camille Marine's Alma is a cropped long-sleeve with a lace-up open back. Not a cutout, not a keyhole — an actual open back with a cord running down the spine. The front is completely plain. All the story is in the back. You'd wear this with high-waisted anything and walk away from everyone in the room.
