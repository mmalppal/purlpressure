Peer Pressure is where other people's knitting gets me into trouble. This round: small scarves. Fourteen of them, from a two-color brioche neckerchief to a ruffled blue collar to a triangle covered in slasher-movie colorwork.

Credit where it's due: most of these came to me via @whattracymakes, @sleepknitting, @squee_creepyed, @epperin, @beef_lover101, and @sayhellotoknits.
